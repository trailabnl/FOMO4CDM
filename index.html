FOMO4CDM Session

Submit a Paper

Overview

Foundation models, i.e., large-scale pre-trained systems that generalize across tasks and modalities, are rapidly transforming clinical AI. This session brings together researchers and beyond to examine their application in clinical decision making through three interconnected lenses: representation (learning from heterogeneous clinical data), clinical reasoning (supporting diagnosis and decision-making), and interaction (enabling meaningful human-AI dialogue in clinical workflows). The session will: (1) showcase state-of-the-art representation learning from multimodal clinical data (EHR, medical imaging, genomics, clinical text); (2) critically examine how foundation models support clinical reasoning, diagnostics, and treatment planning; and (3) explore the design and evaluation of interactive, conversational AI systems deployed in real clinical environments. The session aims to build a durable research community around responsible, high-impact clinical AI.

Foundation models represent the current vanguard of AI and Machine Learning research, bridging core BNAIC themes—representation learning, multi-agent systems, knowledge discovery, reasoning, and human-AI interaction—with a domain of immediate societal urgency. This session is squarely aligned with BNAIC's dual mandate of scientific excellence and real-world impact, positioning the community at the forefront of one of AI's most consequential application areas.
The session is designed for the full breadth of the BNAIC community: principal investigators, postdoctoral researchers, PhD candidates, master's students in medical and/or AI research groups; clinical practitioners and healthcare informaticians; policymakers and regulatory stakeholders; and interdisciplinary researchers. No specific prior expertise in foundation models is assumed; the session is structured to be accessible and stimulating across levels of seniority.

Call for Papers

We are pleased to announce the focused session Foundation Models for Clinical Decision Making (FOMO4CDM) at BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026, taking place on 21–23 October 2026 in Maastricht, the Netherlands.

Foundation models are rapidly changing the landscape of clinical AI. Their potential ranges from learning meaningful representations of complex and heterogeneous health data to supporting clinical reasoning and enabling new forms of interaction between clinicians, patients, and AI systems.

FOMO4CDM aims to bring together researchers working at the intersection of foundation models, machine learning, and healthcare. We invite contributions around three interconnected themes:

  • Representation: Foundation models and representation learning from heterogeneous clinical data, including electronic health records, medical imaging, genomics, clinical text, and multimodal data.
  • Clinical reasoning: Foundation models for diagnosis, prognosis, clinical decision support, treatment planning, and related reasoning tasks.
  • Interaction: Conversational and interactive AI, human-AI collaboration, and the integration of foundation models into clinical workflows.

We also welcome related work on multimodal learning, adaptation and fine-tuning of foundation models, evaluation, interpretability, robustness, fairness, privacy, responsible AI, and real-world clinical deployment.

The session will bring together invited keynote talks, contributed research presentations, and an interactive panel discussion to exchange recent advances, discuss open challenges, and identify future directions for foundation models in clinical decision making. We welcome contributions from researchers and practitioners across artificial intelligence, machine learning, clinical informatics, medical imaging, genomics, natural language processing, clinical decision support, and related fields.

Submission

We invite short papers of up to 6 pages, excluding references, prepared using the Springer LNCS/CCIS single-column format.

Submissions will be handled through OpenReview and reviewed single-blind. Accepted contributions will be included in the BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026 conference proceedings and presented during the FOMO4CDM session.

  • Short paper submission deadline: 31 August 2026
  • Notification of acceptance: 30 September 2026
  • Late-breaking poster submission deadline: 30 September 2026
  • Camera-ready submission deadline: 9 October 2026
  • BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026 conference: 21–23 October 2026

Papers must be submitted via OpenReview, through the general BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026 submission portal (a dedicated FOMO4CDM track is not yet available). Please include “FOMO4CDM” in your keywords or TLDR so that we can identify your submission as intended for this session.
