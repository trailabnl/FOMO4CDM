The Session

Foundation models are rapidly reshaping clinical AI. FOMO4CDM examines their role in clinical decision making through three lenses: representation, clinical reasoning, and interaction. We invite short two-page extended abstracts on ongoing or completed work.

Topics

Representation learning Multimodal clinical data Clinical reasoning & decision support Conversational & interactive AI Human–AI collaboration Adaptation & evaluation Interpretability & robustness Responsible AI: fairness, privacy, safety Real-world deployment
